# Issue 989: console navigation reorganized

The console sidebar now reads, top to bottom: a search box, Catalogs
(Browse, Recent changes, Import & export, then the catalog selector),
Records (Findings, Runs, Changesets, Audit, Unlocks), Administer, and Your
account (My keys, My grants, Account, Billing, Catalogs).

- Search: Enter in the sidebar box goes to `#/search/<query>` (a repeat of
  the same query reads again); a blank box does nothing and sends no request.
  The search page keeps its own form and bounded catalog chooser. The box
  shows the query of a linked or reloaded search.
- Health moved from `pages/health.js` to `components/health.js`
  (`renderHealth`) and is the top section of Recent changes, with its own
  Refresh. Its reads and the revision list's fail independently. `#/health`
  is rewritten to `#/recent`. The default route is Browse.
- Visibility: the Maintenance group is gone; Findings, Runs and Changesets
  are unrestricted as before, Audit and Unlocks keep their gates. The Your
  teams group is gone; its Catalogs link (and the Administer group's) is one
  link in Your account, shown to an admin credential or an account session
  (`data-need="account"`), so API-key admins keep it.
- Tests: `tests/ui_e2e/nav.rs` (order and visibility, search, selector);
  `health.rs`, `smoke.rs`, `self_service.rs`, `unlocks.rs`, `catalogs.rs`
  follow the new selectors.
